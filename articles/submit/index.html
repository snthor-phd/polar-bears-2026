How it works

You write, we post

No account, no app, nothing to install. Fill this in from your phone at the campsite and hit Send entry. It lands in Steve's queue, he approves it, and it shows up on the Trip Journal within a minute or two — your name on it.

Photos are shrunk right here in your browser before they're sent, and all location data is stripped — nothing tells the world where your rig is parked.

A few notes

Worth knowing

  • Signal is thin north of Thompson. Your draft is saved in this browser as you type, so if the send fails you won't lose the writing — reopen this page and it'll still be here.
  • Photos of people: please only send pictures the folks in them would be happy to see on a public website.
  • Typos happen. Send it anyway — small fixes get made before it posts.
