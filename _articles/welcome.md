This is the caravan's trip journal &mdash; the place for dispatches from the road, notes on the stops, and longer articles as the trip unfolds.

As the caravan rolls north from Winnipeg toward Churchill, new entries will appear here automatically, newest first. Each one gets its own page and links back to this list.

For now, the [itinerary]({{ '/itinerary/' | relative_url }}) is the heart of the site &mdash; every day, stop by stop. Check back here once we're underway for stories, photos worth writing about, and any updates the co-leaders send along.

See you on the tundra.
