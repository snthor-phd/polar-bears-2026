Where you sit, where you sleep

Seating and sleeping

What's included

The caravan travels in Economy Plus on the day coach, and that fare is included in the kitty fee. There is also a dome car on the train.

One Economy car seats 68. The seats rotate, so a group of four can face each other, and the foot rests come up — which lets you lie across the seats. The caravan may be seated first, but that's the train manager's call, not a guarantee.

Honest expectation from the co-leaders: nobody got much sleep on the way up, because everyone was too excited. The ride back was much quieter.

If you're in the coach

Bring a pillow and a blanket. The coach gets cool overnight and VIA does not provide bedding in Economy Plus. The caravan hands out black bags to store your pillow and bedding while you're in Churchill, so you're not carrying them around town.

If you booked a sleeper

VIA provides pillows and blankets — you don't need to bring your own. Upgrades were sold round trip as an extra, first come first served, and were arranged through the caravan with Frontiers North. The 2026 inventory ran to one cabin for two (bunk beds with a ladder and its own bathroom), four cabins for one (the bed folds down over the toilet overnight), and two upper/lower berths — the lower berth has a window, the upper does not.

Hard limits

Baggage

TypeAllowance
Carry-on 1 personal item (25 lb · 17 × 6 × 13 in) and 1 item (50 lb · 62 linear in), or 2 items at 25 lb each.
Linear inches = length + width + height.
Checked2 items, 50 lb max each

Checked bags are handled as a group on Oct 2 — they go to the lot at 2:50 PM and to the station at 3:00, so pack the day bag you want with you separately and early. Coming home from Churchill, checked baggage goes outside your room the morning of Oct 6 for transfer, and you keep your carry-on for the day's activities.

A long ride

Comfort and motion sickness

Some past participants have had motion sickness on this run. If you're prone to it, tell the co-leaders as early as you can — they will board you first so you get first pick of the seating, which makes a real difference. Facing forward helps; a window or aisle seat may suit you better than the middle.

As they put it: they want your trip to be as awesome as possible, so it's worth saying something rather than toughing it out.

Bring your own

Food and drink

There is no dining car — but there is a snack and lounge car. It sells beverages, snacks and sandwiches at reasonable prices, and the co-leaders report decent microwaved breakfast items (eggs, sausage, hash browns). The selection is very limited.

The train cannot accommodate special dietary needs. If you have food restrictions, plan on bringing everything you'll want for 16 hours.

  • Pack snacks and the beverages you want for the ride.
  • Alcoholic drinks are sold in the dome car.
  • Anything alcoholic you bring yourself must ride in checked baggage — not in your carry-on.
Before boarding

Security in Thompson

You pass through security screening in Thompson before boarding. Pack your carry-on with that in mind, and leave time for it in the 3:50 PM gathering.

Riding north with us

The donation boxes

The community service donations are sorted in Thompson on Oct 1, and what's bound for Churchill is boxed and loaded onto this train. VIA Rail carries the boxes at no charge to the caravan.
