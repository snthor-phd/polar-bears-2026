Arriving in WinnipegUpdated

The rendezvous is Town & Country Campground on Murdock Road, at the southeast corner of Winnipeg — relocated from Carberry after western-Manitoba flooding and road construction. Full hookups, 30 or 50 amp, water and sewer.

Your parking date, arrival window, and site number came to you individually by email in late August. Windows are staggered rig by rig, so go by the times in your own message rather than a general window. If you need to change your date, tell the co-leaders right away — earlier parking can be accommodated.

Advance parking on Friday, Sep 11 or Saturday, Sep 12 stays available if you arranged it, 1:00–4:00 PM each day, with early parkers on duty. You don't pay the campground directly — the caravan handles it.

The address, and why your GPS may fight you

56001 Murdock Road, Navin, MB. The campground warns that many GPS units don't recognize that address and will route you to the wrong end of Murdock Road. Their workarounds:

  • Set the city to Dugald and enter 870 Murdock Road
  • Some units file the campground under Springfield, MB
  • Or just navigate to the coordinates: 49.8271425, -96.9808513

You're looking for the campground on the left as you head south on Murdock, past Tinkertown Amusement Park and what used to be Fun Mountain Waterpark.

Note the closure: the Murdock Road turn-off from the Perimeter Highway has recently been closed. Coming off the Perimeter you now have to continue past Murdock, take the exit for #1 Hwy West / Fermor Ave, and turn left onto Murdock from there. This applies whether you come in from the west on the TransCanada or up Highway 75 from the US.

Conditions on the ground

No open fires this yearRestriction

Manitoba is extremely dry. No open fires are permitted during the caravan, and burning restrictions are in force across many of the areas the route passes through. The caravan carries a propane fire pit and tank for group use, so evenings around a fire still happen — just not a wood one.

Expect to see wildfire damage along the way. The co-leaders are monitoring conditions and expect things around Thompson to be under control with much improved air quality by the time the caravan arrives, though nobody is guaranteeing weather. Manitoba's province-wide state of emergency has been lifted.

Print, fill out, bring

The Medical Emergency FormRequired

Every person needs their own form in their own sealed envelope, with their name on the outside. Not one per rig — one per person. These are collected on arrival in Winnipeg and travel with the co-leaders everywhere the caravan goes.

The envelopes are opened only in an emergency. Frontiers North Adventures will have access to the information during the Churchill portion of the trip. Your envelope is returned to you before you leave the caravan.

How to mark the envelope

The form itself asks for three things on the outside of the sealed envelope:

  • Your name
  • Your WBCCI number
  • “MEI” printed in the upper left corner

The form asks for date of birth, WBCCI number, medical evacuation company and membership details, primary and secondary insurance with policy numbers, an emergency contact, your physician, and space for medical conditions, allergies, and a full list of current medications (prescription or not).

If a dog is riding along

Crossing the border with a dog

Two separate requirements, one in each direction. This is what previous caravanners were told by their vet and confirmed on the government sites — check the current rules yourself before you leave.

Into Canada — signed rabies certificate

As of July 22, 2024, a rabies vaccination certificate signed by a licensed veterinarian is required. Per inspection.canada.ca, it must:

  • Be in English or French, or come with an official translation
  • Be issued and signed by a licensed veterinarian
  • Identify the animal — age, breed, sex, colour and markings, weight, and microchip or tattoo number if there is one
  • Identify the dog's owner
  • State that the animal is vaccinated against rabies, and give the date of vaccination
  • Give the trade name and serial number of the licensed rabies vaccine
  • Specify the period of validity — otherwise it is treated as valid for one year from the vaccination date
  • Carry the name and signature of the issuing veterinarian and the date signed

All of it has to be legible.

Back into the United States — CDC Dog Import Form

Per the CDC, since August 1, 2024 the CDC Dog Import Form is required to bring your dog into the U.S. Have it before you head home.
