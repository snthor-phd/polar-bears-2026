GAM Hosts

Get Acquainted Meetings — five rounds of small groups, so by The Pas everybody's sat down with everybody. Twenty rigs, five groups of four a night, a different mix each time.

Each GAM splits the caravan into five groups of four. One rig in each group hosts — that's where the other three show up.

Over the five evenings the groups reshuffle, so you meet a new set each time. Most rigs host once, several host twice. Schedule, times and pairings as issued by the caravan leaders — each GAM links to its day in the itinerary.
