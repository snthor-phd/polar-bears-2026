Day 24 · Tuesday, October 6

Dave Daley's dog yard

Wapusk Adventures is Dave Daley's operation on the edge of Churchill, and it is as much a cultural visit as a ride. The session pairs an educational talk on dog sledding, Métis culture, and life in Churchill with a run through the boreal forest behind his team. Wapusk runs about two hours end to end.

This is your last full day in Churchill — the night train south leaves at 7:30 PM.

Set expectations

Cart or sled?

October 6 is autumn in Churchill, not winter. Depending on snow, the team may be hitched to a wheeled cart on a gravel trail rather than a sled on snow. The dogs run the same either way, and the photographs of pristine white trails you have seen from Wapusk were mostly taken later in the season.

Take it as a feature. Fewer layers, easier footing, and the larches are gold that week.

The reason it works

The dogs

These are working northern dogs, not a show kennel — mixed huskies bred for pulling, living outdoors, and visibly keen to run. Visitors consistently come away commenting on how obviously the handlers care about them.

There is normally time in the yard before or after the run to meet the team. Ask before approaching any dog, and let the dog come to you.

One instruction from the co-leaders

What to wear

Dress warm. That is the caravan's own note against this activity, and it is worth taking seriously — you are sitting still in an open cart or sled in Churchill in October, with wind. Hat, gloves, and a windproof outer layer at minimum.

Bring the camera. This is one of the best photo opportunities of the whole trip, and it is over in a couple of hours.
